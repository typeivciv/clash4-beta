v1 playtest

Start with one iPhone and one Android. Test the normal game link in Safari and Chrome so the results reflect what players will open.

Open Clash 4

First session · two phones

  1. Same Wi-Fi: Use the iPhone to host a Direct Duel. Send a fresh invite, finish the match, and try Rematch.
  2. Wi-Fi and cellular: Turn off Wi-Fi on one phone. Have Android host a fresh Direct Duel, then swap who hosts. Record whether each invite worked on the first try or after Retry Connection.
  3. Shared Room: Host a 2–4 Player Room and have Player 2 join. Play through a capture and finish the match. Check that Rematch and Return to Lobby do what their labels say. If more phones are available, add Players 3 and 4 and confirm they see the same match without seeing hidden pieces.

During matches, check that both screens agree on whose turn it is and where pieces land, and that a captured piece disappears when its clash resolves.

Second session · new players

Send the link to two or three people who haven’t played. Let them find Solo Play or Multiplayer, play a first clash, and invite someone without coaching. Record the first spot where they needed help.

What to send back

Send a short note even if everything passed. If a join fails, copy Test Info or Room Diagnostics on both phones before refreshing. During a match, note the first turn where the screens disagreed.
