HOW TO PLAY

CLASH 4

1 · Connect Four

Own four pieces horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Decoys count too.

2 · Choose + Drop

Pick Rock, Paper, Scissors, or Decoy, then choose any legal column.

3 · Combat

Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, and Paper beats Rock. A winner can fall and fight again.

4 · Decoy ○

Decoys never attack or break. Any Decoy contact is a stalemate and stops the chain.

5 · Fog of War

Your identities stay visible. Enemy pieces return to ? after combat, so remembering what you saw matters.

6 · Protection

A decisive R/P/S winner may earn a teal ring. Fortified uses gold; Critical Defense uses amber.

Difficulty

Easy is forgiving, Normal is the family default, Hard uses advanced tactics, and Strategist adds capped look-ahead without cheating.

Board Signals

CLASH marks active combat, the AI preview marks its committed column, and a subtle marker preserves the last move.

Clashmate

A Fortified connected three wins immediately only when the next Connect 4 is truly unavoidable.
